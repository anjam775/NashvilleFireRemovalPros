Contents Cleaning & Pack-Out in Nashville, TN

Furniture, clothing, photos and electronics can often be saved after a fire. A pack-out moves them out, cleans them and stores them until the house is ready.

Quick answer: Contents cleaning and pack-out in Nashville, TN means inventorying your belongings, packing salvageable items, moving them to a cleaning facility, removing soot and smoke odor, and storing them until repairs are done. A detailed inventory with photos also supports the personal property part of your insurance claim.

Your belongings often take more smoke damage than you expect, because soft goods and electronics sit in rooms the fire never reached. Cleaning them in a house that is being torn out and rebuilt is not practical, which is why a pack-out is common after fire damage restoration in Nashville. A local provider can explain which items are worth cleaning and which belong on the loss list.

What contents cleaning and pack-out include

  • A room-by-room inventory with photos, barcodes or labels for each box.
  • Sorting items into salvageable, non-salvageable and needs-testing groups.
  • Careful packing of fragile items, electronics, documents and keepsakes.
  • Transport to a secure, climate-controlled cleaning and storage facility.
  • Cleaning hard goods, furniture, art frames, dishes and décor.
  • Laundry and dry-cleaning for clothing, linens and drapes.
  • Deodorizing contents in an odor chamber where needed.
  • Pack-back and placement of cleaned items when the home is ready.

The pack-out process

  1. Walk-through. The provider and you agree on what goes, what stays and what you want to keep personally.
  2. Inventory. Every item or box is photographed and logged, so you can track it.
  3. Packing. Items are wrapped and boxed by room and labeled for return.
  4. Transport and intake. Contents move to the facility and are checked in.
  5. Cleaning. Each category is cleaned with the right method: ultrasonic, hand wiping, laundering, dry cleaning or deodorizing.
  6. Storage. Cleaned items are stored in a secure space until repairs finish.
  7. Pack-back. Items return and are placed in the rooms they came from.

Cleaning methods for contents

ANSI/IICRC S700 includes contents restoration as part of fire and smoke damage restoration, and providers use a mix of methods:

  • Ultrasonic cleaning uses sound waves in a cleaning tank to remove soot from blinds, dishes, hardware and other hard items.
  • Hand cleaning with dry sponges and mild cleaners suits furniture, frames and décor.
  • Specialty laundering removes soot and odor from clothes, linens and many fabrics.
  • Ozone or hydroxyl chambers deodorize items in a sealed room after cleaning.
  • Freeze-drying recovers documents and photos soaked by firefighting water.
  • Electronics assessment checks devices for soot inside before they are powered on.

What affects the cost of contents work

Cost depends on the number of rooms and boxes, the residue type, how many items need specialty cleaning, storage time and transport distance. Expect a separate line for storage, which grows with the length of the rebuild. Ask how the provider charges for items that turn out to be non-salvageable after testing.

DIY vs. professional contents cleaning

You can safely wash many cotton clothes at home with a strong detergent, though several washes may be needed. Keep valuables, cash, medication and important papers with you. Use a professional for electronics, upholstered furniture, art, heirlooms, large volumes of clothing, or anything with oily soot, since home washing can set stains and spread residue.

Insurance notes for Tennessee

The personal property part of your homeowners or renters policy usually pays for cleaning belongings, or for replacing them when cleaning costs more than the item is worth. The Tennessee Department of Commerce & Insurance notes that contents payments are made to you. Meanwhile, structure payments may include your mortgage company. If you remember items later, tell your insurer, since coverage may still apply. Renters can read the renters insurance and fire damage guide.

Local note: Renters in Murfreesboro near MTSU and apartment residents in Antioch often need contents help most, because the landlord's policy does not cover their belongings. Larger homes in Franklin and Brentwood may need multi-day pack-outs with extra storage.

Contents cleaning and pack-out questions

Can smoke-damaged clothes be saved?

Many can. Professional laundering and dry cleaning remove most soot and odor from clothes and linens. Items with burns or heavy oily soot may not clean fully.

How long are belongings stored during a pack-out?

Usually until repairs are finished, which can be a few weeks for a small fire or several months for a rebuild. Storage time affects the cost.

Should I throw away food after a fire?

Throw away food that was exposed to heat, smoke, soot or firefighting water, including canned goods that bulged or were near heat. Do not taste food to check it.

Are electronics safe to use after smoke exposure?

Not until they are checked. Soot can carry an electrical charge and corrode circuits, so turning devices on too soon can cause more damage.

Does insurance pay for pack-out and storage?

Many homeowners and renters policies cover reasonable costs to protect and clean personal property after a covered fire. Confirm with your adjuster before the pack-out begins.
