Editorial Policy

How the guides on this site are researched, written, checked and kept up to date.

Who writes the content

Guides and pages on this site are written by Nashville Fire Removal Pros Editorial. The team does not claim professional licenses, certifications or credentials it does not hold. If a guide is ever reviewed by a credentialed expert, that reviewer will be named on the guide.

How guides are researched

Each guide starts with the questions people actually search for after a fire, then answers them using primary sources wherever possible:

Tennessee government sources, such as the Department of Commerce and Insurance, the Board for Licensing Contractors and Metro Nashville Codes. Tennessee statutes, cited by section number. National safety and health organizations, such as NFPA, the U.S. Fire Administration, the EPA and the USDA. Industry standards, such as ANSI/IICRC S700 for fire and smoke restoration and IICRC S760 for wildfire smoke. Local reference sources for community facts, such as zip codes and history.

Every guide lists its sources at the end, with a "last reviewed" date.

Accuracy and corrections

Facts, figures and legal rules are checked against the cited source before publishing. If a source cannot confirm a claim, the claim is removed or reworded. When an error is found, it is corrected promptly and the updated date is changed.

Independence

Nashville Fire Removal Pros is a referral service and may be paid when calls are connected to providers. That relationship does not change the facts in the guides. The guides do not recommend specific companies, and they do not promise coverage outcomes, prices or timelines. Learn how the referral service works.

Plain language

Guides use short sentences, define technical terms the first time they appear, and link to the glossary. They are general information, not legal, insurance or professional advice, as explained in the disclaimer.

Updates

Guides are reviewed at least once a year, and sooner when a law, code or standard changes. The "Updated" date on each guide shows the last review.

Images

Photos are stock images chosen to illustrate the topic. They do not show work by any provider in this network.
